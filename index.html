How the week works

Daily XP

Upload each day's spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX with name and completed columns). Students at the goal are marked hit; everyone else in the file is missed.

☀️ By lunch: upload a lunch-time spreadsheet (or tap the sun) for students who already hit the goal. Their axolotl or duck sidekick joins them on the board that day only.

Tap a 120 XP box to fix it by hand: blank → hit → excused.
